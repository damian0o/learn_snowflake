# Learn Snowflake

A three-day foundation in Snowflake for a data engineer moving a team off Cloudera (Hadoop, HDFS, Hive, Spark). Every concept is one note, every note links to its neighbours, and every flashcard and quiz question points back at the note that teaches it. Open the graph view: blue is concepts, green conventions, yellow flashcards, red syllabus, purple memory, orange the Hadoop bridge.
